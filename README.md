## Selected Work

### DailyOps

**Role-based AI employee assistant for everyday desktop workflows**

DailyOps is a local-first desktop assistant designed to reduce repetitive work across different professional roles.

Instead of requiring users to manually open applications, arrange their workspace, switch tools, and repeatedly perform the same actions, DailyOps turns those workflows into reusable operations.

`macOS` `Swift` `Automation` `Voice Control` `AI Agents` `Local-First`

- Role-based environments for developers, managers, and other workflows.
- Voice-triggered actions using push-to-talk controls.
- Launches complete work setups instead of individual applications.
- Supports deterministic local commands that do not require an LLM.
- Designed to optionally extend into agent-driven multi-step workflows.
- Focused on fast, predictable desktop automation rather than chatbot-style interaction.

### Wesley Flow

**Native macOS voice intelligence and command system**

Wesley Flow is a native macOS voice assistant built around fast push-to-talk interaction, local transcription, intelligent formatting, and system-level commands.

`Swift` `SwiftUI` `Speech Recognition` `Voice AI` `Local AI` `macOS`

- Global push-to-talk voice capture.
- Apple Speech, Parakeet, and Whisper transcription engines.
- Custom vocabulary and phrase replacements.
- Automatic punctuation and intelligent text formatting.
- Voice commands for applications and system workflows.
- Dictation history, productivity insights, and per-application analytics.
- Designed around privacy and local processing whenever possible.

### NIYAM — Government Intelligence Platform

**AI-powered government document and policy intelligence**

NIYAM transforms government orders, circulars, notifications, and welfare-scheme documents into structured, searchable information.

`OCR` `RAG` `LLMs` `Policy Intelligence` `FastAPI` `Next.js` `PostgreSQL`

- Extracts structured information from scanned and digital government documents.
- Identifies eligibility rules, benefits, deadlines, and required documents.
- Semantic search across government policies and schemes.
- Tracks amendments and policy-version changes.
- Detects conflicting rules across documents.
- Supports officer-facing review and decision workflows.
- Role-based access for government environments.

**Machine learning for astronomical transit analysis**

Developed during NASA Space Apps Challenge 2025 using Kepler and TESS astronomical data.

`Machine Learning` `Astronomy` `Python` `Data Analysis` `Visualization`

- Achieved **94.8% precision** on the evaluation dataset.
